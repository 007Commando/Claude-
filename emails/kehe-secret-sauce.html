3 things most sellers miss

KeHE is a multi-billion dollar distributor. Most sellers only ever see a fraction of what it can do for them. Here's the concept behind each opportunity, and exactly what to do about it.

01  ·  MULTIPLY YOUR CATALOG

KeHE's warehouses aren't identical

THE CONCEPT

KeHE runs warehouses across the country that they call “districts.” Each district stocks its own mix of inventory, so the products available to you depend on which warehouse actually services your ship-to address.

THE MOVE

Once your KeHE account is open, reach out to our prep center network and open ship-to locations in other states. Each new district you unlock can add 3–5x more merchandise than what your home district alone would ever show you.

02  ·  UNLOCK BETTER PRICING

Your rep decides your discount

THE CONCEPT

KeHE has five price tiers, and where you land isn't just about volume, it's a relationship that KeHE's sales reps manage directly.

THE MOVE

Stay in regular contact with your rep, communicate what you're buying and why, and keep ordering consistently. Sellers who treat their rep as a relationship instead of an order form are the ones who get moved up.

03  ·  SPOT THE NEXT BIG BRAND

Follow what KeHE is pushing

THE CONCEPT

KeHE is a promotional distributor. Many of their most profitable items are ones they're actively “pushing” for the week or month on behalf of a brand trying to earn shelf space.

THE MOVE

Use that signal two ways: stock up on the discounted inventory while it's promoted, and reach out directly to the brand behind it. Getting in early with a growing brand is one of the fastest paths to brand registry and long-term exclusivity.
