# Desktop remarketing: get account holders to connect Seller Central

Written 2026-09-27 after Stefano asked whether Taboola could put Apex "in
front of the right people when they are on their desktop so they can easily
navigate to clicking and connecting to their Seller Central". The answer was
no: the right people are already account holders, and the moment is a desktop
session. This is that campaign.

## The audience

Accounts that exist but have not connected Amazon. 89% of accounts, per the
lifecycle project's Firestore count. They signed up on a phone (Facebook lead
forms, PrimeWell's mobile site) and the Seller Central OAuth is miserable on
a phone, so they stopped.

Built from the Google Ads tag in the app (frontend branch
`feat/google-ads-tag`, env var set in hosting when it ships). Events:

- `page_view` on every route, so "visited app.apexapplications.io" fills.
- `app_session` with `amazon_connected: true|false` once per session.
- `amazon_connect_started` on the Connect click.
- `amazon_connected` on OAuth success.

Google Ads' own audience builder works on URLs, not custom events (those
need a GA4 property the app tag deliberately does not have), so the tag also
reports each session as a page view of a virtual path:

- `/__audience/amazon-not-connected` when the account has no Amazon access
- `/__audience/amazon-connected` when it has, and again on OAuth success

Segments in Google Ads (Tools > Audience manager > Your data), all "Website
visitors", rule "URL contains":

| Segment | Rule | Window |
|---|---|---|
| App: Amazon not connected | `/__audience/amazon-not-connected` | 30 days |
| App: Amazon connected | `/__audience/amazon-connected` | 540 days |
| App visitors (any) | `app.apexapplications.io` | 30 days |

Campaign audience: "App: Amazon not connected" targeted, "App: Amazon
connected" excluded. Until the app deploy ships the lists stay empty and
"App visitors (any)" is the stand-in.

Not Customer Match. The account is weeks old and Customer Match needs policy
history first; revisit at 90 days.

## The campaign

- Type: Display, standard (not Performance Max, which would ignore the audience).
- Audience: the segment above, **targeting** not observation, no expansion,
  optimised targeting OFF (otherwise Google adds strangers).
- Devices: **desktop only**. Mobile and tablet bid adjustment -100%.
- Location United States, language English.
- Bidding: Manual CPC, $0.60 to start; the list is small, frequency matters
  more than bid. Frequency cap 3 per day per user.
- Budget: $5/day. The list is a few hundred people; more buys nothing.
- Conversion: Trial Started primary as everywhere else; Amazon connect is
  not a Google conversion, it is read in the app.
- Landing page: the Amazon connect route in the app (from the frontend
  branch report). Signed-out users hit the login and return there.
- Tracking template: same shape as search,
  `{lpurl}?utm_source=google&utm_medium=display&utm_campaign=rmkt-connect-desktop&utm_content={creative}&device={device}`

## The copy

One message. They already have the account; the ad's only job is to make the
connect feel like a two-minute task and to be seen at a desk.

Headlines (30):
- Connect Seller Central in 2 minutes
- Your Apex account is waiting
- Finish setup on your computer
- Sync your Amazon sales to Apex
- See profit per SKU by tomorrow
- One click to connect Amazon

Long headline (90):
- Connect Seller Central to Apex in two minutes and see profit, fees and restock needs by tomorrow.

Descriptions (90):
- You created the account. Connecting Amazon is the one step left, and it takes two minutes at a desk.
- Once connected, Apex pulls your orders, fees and inventory and shows what each SKU actually earns.
- Nothing to install. Sign in on your computer, click Connect, approve in Seller Central. Done.

Business name: Apex Applications
Call to action: Connect now

Images (in `marketing/google-ads/creative/`, generated by `creative.py`):
- `rmkt-1200x628.png` landscape 1.91:1
- `rmkt-1200x1200.png` square 1:1
- `logo-1200x1200.png` square logo, `logo-1200x300.png` 4:1 logo

## What would make this unnecessary

The "Email me a link to finish on my computer" button (backend + frontend
branch `feat/finish-on-desktop`). If that moves the connect rate, the ad is a
backstop for people who ignored the email. Read both before spending on the
ad: connect rate for accounts created after the button ships vs before.
