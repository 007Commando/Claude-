# Video scripts

Ten scripts. Each one names the campaign it belongs to in `03-youtube.md`.

**Two rules before anything is filmed.**

**No rival is named, in the voiceover or on screen.** Google's trademark policy
governs ad text, and on-screen text and the headline and description assets are
ad text. Spoken comparative claims are lawful when they are true, but there is
no version of this where naming them is worth an account-level complaint from
a competitor. Every script below makes the comparison without the name, which
is also a better ad: "the tool you are using" lets the viewer fill in their own.

**Every number spoken is checked against `src/config/offer.ts` before it is
recorded.** Today that means Starter is $149 a month, the trial is seven days,
the card is collected up front and is not charged, the repricer is in every
plan, and every account gets three vetted US distributors on sign up plus three
more each month. If `NEW_PRICING_LIVE` flips, the videos quoting $149 are
retired the same day.

Delivery: 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 for all of them. Burned-in captions always.

## Text assets for every video ad

Same constraint as search: 30 characters for a headline, 90 for a description,
and no rival named.

**Headlines**
```
One Suite, Not Five Tabs
Repricer In Every Plan
Scan, Buy, Price, Restock
Your Floor Is Your Real Cost
$149/mo For The Whole Suite
7 Days Free, No Charge Yet
3 Distributors On Sign Up
See An Honest Comparison
```

**Descriptions**
```
Scanning, purchase orders, repricing and profit in one place. Seven days free.
Your price floor comes from your real landed cost, not a number you keep by hand.
An honest comparison, including where the other tool wins. Real pricing, no spin.
Card up front, nothing charged for seven days. Cancel before it ends, pay nothing.
```

**Call to action buttons:** Start free trial, See comparison, Learn more.
Never "Buy now", which is false for a trial and reads as pressure.

## Production order

If only three get made, make these three, in this order:

1. **CONQ-30, the floor.** Thirty seconds, one screen recording, and it is the
   only argument in this folder that no competitor can copy quickly.
2. **DEMO-90.** It is the product doing the selling, and it recuts into six
   shorter assets.
3. **RMK-15.** Cheapest to make, and it is aimed at people who already got as
   far as the checkout.
