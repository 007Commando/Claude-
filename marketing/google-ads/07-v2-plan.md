# Google Search v2: high intent, clear call to action (October 2026)

Generated by `build_v2.py` into `import/v2/`. Everything is created **paused**;
nothing spends until a campaign is switched on.

## Why v2

v1 (S01 to S10, built 2026-09-23) ran from 2026-09-27 with three campaigns on
at $30 a day. Sep 30 to Oct 6: about 93 impressions, 4 clicks, $21.62, no
trial. Three things were wrong with it:

1. **Its ads claimed things the product does not do.** "Repricer In Every
   Plan" and "$149/mo For The Whole Suite" sat in the live brand ad. The
   repricer is Pro ($299), in beta, and moves prices only when the seller runs
   their strategies. The live Repricing campaign sold that product to people
   searching "amazon repricer", who expect it to reprice on its own.
2. **A third of its ad groups landed on reading pages** (ungating guide,
   review automation explainer, prep center directory, an ad funnel), and
   none of the 18 comparison pages had a trial button above the fold. Fixed in
   site commit 57f2f63; every v2 landing page was audited at 1440 and 390 wide.
3. **Bare competitor names** ("sellerboard") are mostly people looking for
   that tool's login. They do not switch.

## Rules the build enforces

1. Final URLs only from the approved list; `--check-live` fetches each and
   requires HTTP 200 and the "Start my 7-day trial" button.
2. Exact and phrase only; no DKI.
3. No competitor name in ad text; no bare competitor keyword.
4. No unsupported claim ("every plan", "whole suite", "real time", "guarantee",
   "win the buy box" and more); 30/90/15 limits; no dash punctuation.
5. A price only where the landing page prints it (`/`, `/pricing`).
6. No keyword blocked by its own campaign's negatives.

## Bidding

Manual CPC to start (no conversion history yet), opening bids near Keyword
Planner's top-of-page range. Trial Started (AW-17638589911) is the only
primary conversion. After about 15 trials, switch each campaign to Maximize
conversions; after about 30, set a target CPA (opening target $100 per trial,
see `01-strategy.md`).

## Launch order

1. Deploy the site (57f2f63 adds the above-the-fold trial buttons), then run
   `python3 build_v2.py --check-live` against production. It must pass.
2. Pause v1's live campaigns (S01 Brand Defence, S05 Repricing, S06
   Wholesale Scanning): their ads carry the false claims above.
3. Upload `import/v2/01` to `05` in order: Tools > Bulk actions > Uploads.
4. Switch on A1, A2, A3 and A4 first; A5 once the AI guides are click-tested.
5. Week 1: read the search terms report daily, add negatives, check that
   Trial Started fires on real trials. Week 2: move budget to whichever ad
   groups produce trials.

## Measurement

- Cost per Trial Started by ad group (Google Ads), against $100.
- Trial to paid from Stripe via Lead Desk (utm_campaign = the campaign slug,
  e.g. `a2-wholesale-software`).
- Known gap: `/api/track` answers 503 in production (no Supabase keys on
  Vercel), so the gclid is not stored and the offline Paid Customer import has
  nothing to key on until the keys are added.
